# ADC quick reference

**0.1.0-draft.1 · Informative · [Normative specification](ADC-SPEC.md)**

Preserve enough meaning for another capable participant to continue or evaluate the work. Use existing records; these are questions to answer, not required fields or files.

## Before acting

- Know the purpose, desired outcome or question, scope, and basis for evaluation.
- Establish applicable constraints and authority. Identify assumptions, risks, and unknowns that could change the action; limit action accordingly.
- Distinguish the current observed state from plans and old observations.

## During work

- Preserve material discoveries, effects, limitations, and changes to intent or criteria.
- Record consequential decisions: choice, reason, and consequences. Routine reversible choices need no separate record.
- Connect material claims to their basis. Identify the observation or judgment, source/method, result, applicable target and conditions, and meaningful limits.
- Reuse existing information. Private reasoning and complete histories are not required.

## When work stops or changes hands

Leave an identifiable current account: intent; resulting state and outputs; supported findings; unresolved matters; and continuation or closure basis. Include unfinished external operations and recovery obligations when relevant.

| Meaning | What remains clear |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Work remains intended; next action or condition, or explicitly undetermined. |
| Suspended | Deliberately paused; why and what permits resumption, including if unknown. |
| Completed | Which scope's completion conditions were satisfied, how evaluated, actual outcome, limits, and outside-scope follow-up. |
| Abandoned | Scope will not be completed under this intent; why, partial results, and residue. |
| Superseded | What replaces which record, decision, claim, or scope; replacement does not prove implementation. |

Labels and transition order are not prescribed. Failure is a result, not necessarily cessation. A completed investigation may be inconclusive; a completed attempt does not establish a successful intervention.

## Before claiming an outcome

- Match the claim's breadth to its evidence. A caveat cannot rescue an unsupported success claim.
- Distinguish observed facts, inference, proposals, and unknowns. Disclose material contrary evidence and unexamined scope.
- Do not turn planned checks into results, historical evidence into current verification, or a decision into implemented behavior.
- Narrow or leave unresolved anything the available basis cannot establish.

## Add rigor when it addresses a need

Consider impact, reversibility, uncertainty, and dependencies. High-consequence work needs an explicit assurance basis, relevant authority, residual risks, and applicable recovery or containment. Independent checking is useful where shared assumptions could hide consequential error—not as universal ceremony.

## Conformance check

Can a capable newcomer locate and interpret the account without hidden reasoning or ADC-specific software? Are current authority, evidence limits, and replacement relationships clear? Empty templates do not count. One short existing record can suffice. Claim **ADC 0.1.0-draft.1 core** only for an identified scope and point in time that meets the specification.
